# Munim — 30 s promo film (16:9)

## Treatment
- **Promise:** Munim closes the weekly cash gap — you get paid before the supplier is due.
- **Identity:** The app's bahi-khata palette: warm paper #F7F1E6, indigo ink #22285F, haldi #F2A51A, owed-green #1C6E48, owe-red #A92530. Fraunces (display serif), Mukta (body, Devanagari), Noto Sans Kannada, JetBrains Mono (numbers).
- **Hero:** The ledger line — a single indigo ink stroke that is the week's cash. It dips red, then is pulled back to green by Munim.
- **Motion:** stroke-traced ledger ink, stamp-press accents (scale 1.6 → 1 with rotate), masked type lifts. Global camera drifts per scene.
- **Arc:** quiet evening counter → anxiety of the dip → hands-free input → agent drafts, merchant approves → payments land, line climbs → calm indigo lockup.
- **Payoff:** ending inverts to an indigo field with the haldi "म" seal stamped like a ledger mark.
