# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Small Indian business owners: kirana shops, tiffin services, tailors, carts, home side hustles. They use a mid-range Android phone, one-handed, at the counter between customers, often in bright daylight. Many are more fluent in Kannada or Hindi than English. Their customers (who receive reminders and open the pay page) are ordinary UPI users on any phone.

## Product Purpose
Munim closes the weekly cash gap: the merchant pays suppliers before customers pay them. The merchant tells Munim what happened (voice, photo of the khata page, text), Munim forecasts the gap, an agent picks who to remind and drafts polite messages, the merchant approves, customers pay through a Pine Labs link. Success: the merchant gets paid before the supplier is due, without awkward conversations.

## Brand Personality
Trusted, calm, quick. Like an old family munim: respectful, knows the numbers, never pushy. Money states are always unambiguous: green means owed to you, red means you owe or are short.

## Anti-references
- Generic fintech dashboards with charts nobody reads.
- Khatabook-style dense ledgers that record but never tell you what to do.
- Anything that looks like a loan app or debt collector: aggressive red alerts, threatening copy.

## Design Principles
1. One answer per screen: the merchant should know the next action in two seconds.
2. Human approves money: every outgoing message and link is visibly the merchant's choice.
3. Big, thumb-sized targets; no typing where a tap, photo or voice works.
4. Honest numbers: forecasts say "likely", test mode says test mode, nothing is labelled verified unless it is.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast (outdoor screens), touch targets ≥44px, readable at 390px width, Indic scripts must render (Kannada, Hindi). Respect reduced motion.
